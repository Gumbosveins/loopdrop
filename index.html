Loopdrop

Match colors. Loop the track. Clear the cubes.

How to Play

  • Tap a colored drop onto the track. As it loops, it can pop one matching cube per grid line per lap — outermost from that side.
  • Track holds up to 5 drops. When a drop finishes a lap with ammo left, it parks in a holding slot. Full slots = fail.
  • Clear every cube to win. Manage ammo packets and slots — that's the puzzle.
